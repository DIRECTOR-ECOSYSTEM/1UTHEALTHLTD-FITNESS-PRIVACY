1UTHEALTHLTD® FITNESS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

1UTHEALTHLTD® FITNESS (“Fitness”, “the App”) is operated by 1UT LTD (“1UT”, “we”, “us”, or “our”).

This Privacy Policy explains how 1UTHEALTHLTD® FITNESS accesses, collects, uses, stores, protects and deletes information when you use the App.

1. Information we may process

Depending on the features you choose to use, Fitness may process:

Account and profile information

This may include information you provide when creating or maintaining an account or profile, such as your name, email address, fitness preferences, goals and profile settings.

Fitness and workout information

This may include:

  • workouts and exercises completed;
  • sets, repetitions and duration;
  • workout history;
  • training plans and templates;
  • achievements, XP, ranks and streaks;
  • body measurements or weight information you choose to record;
  • nutrition and hydration information you choose to enter.

Health Connect information

If you voluntarily connect Fitness to Android Health Connect and grant permission, Fitness may access the specific health and fitness data types for which you grant permission.

Fitness only requests Health Connect permissions relevant to supported Fitness functionality.

You control Health Connect permissions through Android and can withdraw them at any time.

Health Connect data is not sold and is not used for advertising, creditworthiness, insurance eligibility, employment eligibility or data-broker purposes.

Camera and Form Vision

If you voluntarily use camera-based Form Vision features, Fitness may access camera input to provide the requested fitness/form-analysis functionality.

Camera access is controlled through Android permissions and is not required for ordinary workout tracking.

2. How we use information

Information may be used to:

  • provide Fitness functionality;
  • generate and personalise workouts;
  • track fitness progress;
  • provide exercise, training, nutrition and hydration features;
  • provide Health Connect functionality;
  • provide camera-based Form Vision functionality;
  • save preferences and settings;
  • maintain security;
  • diagnose technical problems;
  • improve reliability;
  • comply with legal obligations.

We do not use sensitive health or fitness information for unrelated purposes.

3. Health Connect

Fitness accesses Health Connect only when you choose to connect it and grant the relevant permissions.

Fitness does not request access to health information merely because it exists on your device.

Health Connect data is not sold to third parties and is not used for:

  • targeted advertising;
  • creditworthiness;
  • insurance eligibility;
  • employment eligibility;
  • data-broker activities;
  • unrelated profiling.

You can manage Fitness's Health Connect permissions through Android Health Connect.

4. Camera and Form Vision

Camera functionality is optional.

When activated, the camera is used for the Form Vision functionality explained to you at the point of use.

Camera access is not intended for unrelated surveillance or advertising.

You can control camera access through Android permission settings.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information or sensitive health and fitness information.

Information may be processed or disclosed where necessary to:

  • provide functionality you request;
  • operate essential technical services;
  • maintain security and reliability;
  • comply with law or lawful legal processes;
  • protect users and the service.

Third-party service providers processing information on our behalf are expected to process it only for legitimate service purposes and in accordance with applicable requirements.

6. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide relevant functionality, maintain security, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements.

Information no longer required will be deleted or securely disposed of where reasonably practicable.

7. Account and data deletion

Where Fitness provides account creation, users can request deletion of their account and associated personal information by contacting:

privacy@1uthealthltd.com

You may also contact this address to request information about personal-data processing or exercise applicable privacy rights.

Deleting the App from your device does not necessarily delete information held by an online service.

Health Connect permissions can be withdrawn through Android Health Connect.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

Where applicable, data transmitted between the App and supporting services uses secure transport mechanisms such as HTTPS/TLS.

No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

For security-related concerns, contact: security@1uthealthltd.com

9. Children's privacy

Fitness is not specifically directed at children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law.

Concerns regarding children's information can be reported to: privacy@1uthealthltd.com

10. International processing

Depending on the services used to operate Fitness, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live.

Where applicable, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers and processing.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to:

  • access information;
  • request correction;
  • request deletion;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • withdraw consent where applicable;
  • request information about how your data is used.

For privacy and data-protection requests: privacy@1uthealthltd.com

For legal enquiries: legal@1uthealthltd.com

For compliance matters: compliance@1uthealthltd.com

For users in the United Kingdom, the relevant supervisory authority is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Fitness, its features, technology or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be made available at the public Privacy Policy URL associated with Fitness.
