Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Request deletion

If you have an 1UTHEALTHLTD® FITNESS account and want your account and associated personal data deleted, email privacy@1uthealthltd.com from the email address associated with your account.

Subject: 1UTHEALTHLTD FITNESS — Account Deletion Request

What happens

We will verify the request as reasonably necessary, process the deletion of the account and associated personal data that we are required or able to delete, and communicate any information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or other lawful reasons.

Health and fitness data

Where applicable, deletion requests cover personal fitness information held by the app. Health Connect data is handled in accordance with Google Play and Health Connect requirements and applicable permissions. Data that remains solely in a user's device or in a third-party service may require deletion through that service's own controls.

Privacy policy

For full information about data collection, use, sharing, retention and rights, see the 1UTHEALTHLTD® FITNESS Privacy Policy.
